Cutting Force

How it is calculated
  1. Radial immersion ar = ae/D · engagement φ = acos(1−2ar)
  2. Mean chip hm = 2·ar·fz/φ·sinκr
  3. Specific force (Kienzle) kc = kc1.1·hm−mc
  4. Tangential Ft = kc·ae·ap·fz·z/(πD); Fr=0.4Ft; Fa=0.3Ft; Fres=√(Ft²+Fr²+Fa²)
  5. Power Pc=Ft·vc/60; Torque=Ft·(D/2)/1000
  6. Deflection δ=FbendL³/(3EI), I=π(0.8D)⁴/64, carbide E=600 GPa
  7. Ranges span the material rake band [1−(γ−γref)·0.01]
